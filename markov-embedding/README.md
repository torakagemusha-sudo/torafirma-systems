Written mathematical arguments for exponential metric kernels on at most four points and a five-state deleted-corner obstruction family.

## Evidence and limitations

The headline theorem chain is not end-to-end Lean-formalized. Historical Lean checks cover algebraic components; no fresh Lean build is asserted for this revision. Finite numerical regressions supplement the written proofs and are not universal proofs. A complete classification of five-state kernels remains open.
